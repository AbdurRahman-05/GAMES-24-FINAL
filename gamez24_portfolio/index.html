Frequently asked operational questions.

How does the Turf Owner ₹1,180/mo subscription work?
Venue owners pay a flat ₹1,000 + 18% GST (₹1,180 total) per month for unlimited bookings, QR gate ticket validation, schedule maintenance, and player discovery. Gamez24 charges zero commission on individual player bookings.
What occurs when an operator sets a slot to "Already Booked"?
When a customer reserves via phone call or walks in with cash, the operator selects "Already Booked". The slot turns blue on the owner dashboard and is instantly removed from the Player App, preventing duplicate reservations.
Can operators configure separate Weekday, Weekend, Day, and Night tariffs?
Yes. In the Operating Timings modal, operators establish independent hourly rates for Weekdays (Monday through Friday), Weekends (Saturday and Sunday), Day hours (06:00 AM – 06:00 PM), and Night floodlight hours (06:00 PM – 12:00 AM).
Where can I inspect the Google Play Developer privacy policy?
The full compliance policy is published at privacy-policy.html. It strictly complies with Google Play Developer Data Safety guidelines regarding user contacts, scan telemetry, and payment security.
