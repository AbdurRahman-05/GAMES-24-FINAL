Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Turf Owner ₹1,180/mo subscription work?

Venue owners pay a flat ₹1,000 + 18% GST (₹1,180 total) per month for unlimited bookings, QR barcode validation, schedule management, and instant player discovery. Gamez24 charges zero commission on individual customer match bookings.

What happens when an owner marks a slot as "Already Booked"?

If a customer books via direct phone call or walks in with cash, the owner taps the slot, selects "Already Booked", and confirms. The slot card turns blue in the owner app and is instantly locked from the Player App so no double-booking can ever occur.

Can owners configure separate Weekday, Weekend, Day, and Night rates?

Yes! In the Operating Timings modal, owners set independent tariffs for Weekdays (Mon - Fri), Weekends (Sat - Sun), Day slots (06:00 AM - 06:00 PM), and Night floodlight slots (06:00 PM - 12:00 AM).

Where can I find the Google Play Privacy Policy?

Our full privacy policy is published at privacy-policy.html. It complies with all Google Play Developer Program policies regarding data safety, contact info, and permission handling.
