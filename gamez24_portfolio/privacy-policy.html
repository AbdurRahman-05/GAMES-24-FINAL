Google Play Store & Ecosystem Policy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2026 • Effective Date: November 14, 2026

1. Scope & Ecosystem Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, and disclosure of personal and device data through applications and services operated under the Gamez24 brand:

  • Gamez24 Player App (com.gamez24.player): Sports court slot booking, QR ticket verification, matchmaking, and player tournament participation.
  • Gamez24 Turf Owner App (com.gamez24.ownerapp): Turf venue operations, pricing schedule & tariffs, gate pass QR scanning, offline manual bookings, and payout records.
  • Gamez24 Web & Administration Portals (gamez24.in): Superadmin venue audit, KYC validation, and developer operations.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information Provided by Users

  • Contact Information: Full name, phone number, and optional email for OTP authentication and booking receipts.
  • Venue Owner Business & Tax Data: Turf name, physical address, business PAN, GSTIN number, Aadhaar reference, and bank account / UPI VPA for automated settlement.
  • Booking Details: Selected sports, booked slot timings, payment status, and player attendance history.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & OS Identifiers: Operating system version, device model, app version, and crash logs to troubleshoot performance.
  • Coarse & Precise Location: Used with explicit runtime consent to display nearby sports grounds and driving directions.
  • Scan Metadata: Timestamp and ticket cryptographic verification token upon gate pass check-in.

3. App Permissions & Purpose Specification

Permission App Component Purpose Justification
android.permission.CAMERA Turf Owner App Required strictly for the on-device QR scanner to validate customer gate passes at the venue entrance. Images or video streams are never uploaded or stored.
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION Player App Enables players to discover nearby arenas in Madurai and South India, calculate travel distance, and trigger geo-validated venue check-in.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Player & Owner Apps Dispatches real-time booking confirmations, slot cancellation alerts, reminder timers, and venue maintenance notifications.

4. Data Processing, Storage & Security

We adopt rigorous industry security standards:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between mobile clients, web portals, and backend APIs are encrypted using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive banking details, PAN credentials, and hashes are protected in our PostgreSQL database using state-of-the-art encryption algorithms.
  • No Resale of Data: We never sell, lease, or monetize user data or booking habits to third-party ad brokers or analytics networks.

5. Payment Gateways & Financial Transactions

Online bookings and platform operator licenses (₹1,180/month inclusive of 18% GST) are processed via RBI-compliant payment aggregators (e.g. Razorpay / UPI). Gamez24 does not capture or store debit/credit card numbers or CVV codes directly on its application servers.

6. User Rights & Account Deletion Policy

Under Google Play Store policies and international data protection standards, every user retains full control over their personal data:

  • Right to Access & Correct: You may update your profile or business details at any time in the app settings.
  • Right to Erasure / Account Deletion: You may request the permanent removal of your account, active profiles, and booking history by emailing us at privacy@gamez24.in or tapping Delete Account in your profile menu. All non-regulatory personal records are deleted within 30 days.
